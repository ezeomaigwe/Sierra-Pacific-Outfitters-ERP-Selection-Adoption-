# Sierra-Pacific-Outfitters-ERP-Selection-Adoption-
Led technical analysis comparing planned vs. actual ERP pilot outcomes using the ADKAR change-management model; identified user adoption (not platform selection or data migration) as the primary source of schedule and budget variance, informing contingency planning for enterprise-wide rollout.
A graduate capstone consulting project, evaluating enterprise resource planning (ERP) platforms for Sierra Pacific Outfitters, a fictional retailer whose growth had outpaced its disconnected systems (separate tools for accounting, inventory, POS, e-commerce, and wholesale, causing duplicate data entry, a 3-week financial close, and poor executive visibility). The team evaluated three candidate platforms, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, Oracle NetSuite, and SAP Business One, using a weighted requirements matrix, and recommended Business Central (score 4.40/5) based on its balance of functionality, security, vendor stability, and cost within a $400K–$750K planning envelope.
